Notible is a notes app that keeps your work on your own computer. It starts as a place to write, and grows into projects, tasks and boards when you need them — most of that comes from plugins you can turn on or off.

## Your first note

Press **+** at the top of the sidebar and choose **New note**, or press `Ctrl+N`. Type a title, then write underneath it. Notible saves as you type; there is no Save button.

## The + menu

**+** creates everything that lives in your sidebar: notes, folders, projects, workspaces, whiteboards, tasks and issues. Each item in the menu says in one line what it is for. Folders, projects and workspaces hold other items — each is meant for something different, explained in [Organising your work](help:containers).

![The + menu](help/plus-menu.webp)

## The / menu

Inside a note, type `/` to open a menu of blocks: headings, bullet and numbered lists, checklists, quotes, code, columns, dividers and diagrams. Keep typing to filter the list, then press Enter.

![Adding a checklist from the / menu](help/slash-menu.webm)

Some blocks come from plugins, and appear only while that plugin is on.

## Where things are

- **The sidebar** on the left is your tree of notes, folders and projects, under the heading **All notes**. On a narrow window it waits behind the menu button at the top left.
- **Search** is the magnifier next to **+** (or `Ctrl+K`). It finds notes, settings and pages of this Help.
- **Help** — what you are reading — is at the bottom of the sidebar, or press `F1` anywhere.
- **Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar: appearance, languages, hotkeys, plugins, recovery and updates.
