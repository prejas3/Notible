Adds whiteboards: freeform canvases of cards, shapes and sections that you drag around and connect with lines — for mapping ideas, or how things relate, instead of describing it in a list.

![Dragging a card on a whiteboard](help/whiteboard-drag.webm)

Create one with **+ → Whiteboard**, or add a **Whiteboard** tab to a folder, project or workspace under **Modules**.

**On by default.**

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
