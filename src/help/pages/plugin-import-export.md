Exports a single note or project — with everything inside it — as one file, and imports such a file back. Use it to move one piece of work to another workspace or hand it to someone, without sharing your whole workspace.

Notible always has **Export JSON (with children)** in a row's right-click menu under **Export and print**, and **Import here…** in the right-click menu of a folder, project or workspace. Turning this plugin on adds the **Import and export** tool in Settings → Plugins, where you pick what to export. Whole-workspace backups are a separate feature in Settings → Recovery; see [Move, export and delete](help:move-export-delete).

**Off by default.**

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
