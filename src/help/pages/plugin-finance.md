Adds financial entries (costs and income) and time entries (hours, billable or not, rates, currency) to projects, so you can see the budget left without a spreadsheet.

The figures appear in a project's **Finance** tab, which comes with [Planning](help:plugin-planning).

**Off by default.** Needs [Properties](help:plugin-properties).

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
