Connects a project's **Calendar** tab to Google or Outlook, so its dated work shows next to your meetings. Sign in and pick calendars under **Connections** in that tab; events from those calendars are read-only.

It also turns the reminder set on a local calendar event into a notification when it comes due.

**Off by default.** The Calendar tab itself comes with [Planning](help:plugin-planning). Reading your calendars also needs **Let plugins read your calendars** in Settings → Files & links.

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
