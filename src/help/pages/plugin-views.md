Adds the saved views that projects and workspaces show in their tabs:

- a project's **Tasks** tab — the same items as a board, list, table, calendar or gallery, with filters and grouping;
- a workspace's **Table** (everything across its containers) and **Overview** (each project's progress, this week's work, recent activity).

Each view remembers its layout, filters and sorting. See [Properties and views](help:properties-and-views).

**On by default.** Works with [Tasks, Issues and Projects](help:plugin-work-models).

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
