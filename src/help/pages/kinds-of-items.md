Besides containers, the **+** menu creates items. Each kind is a note underneath — you can always write in it — plus the fields that kind needs, shown in the Properties panel on the right.

## Notes

Plain writing: meeting notes, ideas, documentation. No required fields. You can still add a one-off property to a single note ([Properties](help:plugin-properties)).

## Tasks

Something to do. Fields include **status** (from triage and backlog through in progress to done), **priority**, **due** and **start** dates, a **reminder**, **recurrence** and tags. Tasks inside a project appear on its board.

## Issues

A problem to track, such as a bug. Like a task, but with a **severity** and a status that ends in **resolved** or **closed**.

## Whiteboards

A canvas instead of a page: cards, shapes and connectors you arrange freely. See [Whiteboard](help:plugin-whiteboard).

## Which one?

Write a **note** when you want to record something, a **task** when someone has to do it, an **issue** when something is wrong, and a **whiteboard** when the relationships matter more than the text.

Tasks, issues and projects come from [Tasks, Issues and Projects](help:plugin-work-models); plugins can add more kinds, such as a manuscript from [Typewriter](help:plugin-typewriter).
