Adds the **Properties** panel on the right of notes, tasks and issues: status, priority, assignee, dates and any other field its kind defines. Projects, folders and workspaces don't have one.

It also lets you add a one-off property to a single item — text, a date, a link, a number or a checkbox — without changing its type. Give one meeting note a "decision due" date, and it stays on that note alone.

**On by default.** Turning it off removes the whole side panel, custom properties included.

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
