## Properties

The panel on the right of an item shows its **properties**: status, priority, dates, tags and whatever else its kind defines. Change them there. **+ Add property** adds a one-off field to just this item. See [Properties](help:plugin-properties).

## Views

A project's **Tasks** tab shows its items through a view. Switch the layout at any time:

| Layout | Good for |
|---|---|
| Kanban | Moving work between statuses |
| List | A quick scan |
| Table | Comparing fields side by side |
| Calendar | Seeing what is due when |
| Gallery | Cards with more room |

Above the view you can **search** inside it, **group** by a field, and add **filters**. **+ New view** saves another view next to it; **Duplicate** copies the current one. Each view remembers its own settings.

## Import and export CSV

Open **⋯** above the view. **Export CSV** saves the items in the current view as a spreadsheet file; **Import CSV** creates items from one, each row becoming an item.

Views come from [Views and Dashboards](help:plugin-views).
