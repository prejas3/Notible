Besides the plugins built into Notible, you can install more from the market: **Settings → Plugins → Market**.

- **Official** plugins are published by the Notible developer.
- **Community** plugins are published by other people and are not reviewed by Notible. You install them at your own risk.

Installed plugins update from the same screen when a new version is out.

## Where to find a plugin's instructions

Each market plugin documents itself. Open Settings → Plugins, pick the plugin, and read its **Instructions**. This Help covers Notible itself and its built-in plugins.

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
