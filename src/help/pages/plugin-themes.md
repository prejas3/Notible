Lets you install and switch community themes: style-only packs that change colours, backgrounds, glass effects and animations. You can go back to the built-in Daylight or Midnight at any time.

Once it is on, pick a theme in Settings → Plugins → **Themes**.

**Off by default.**

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
