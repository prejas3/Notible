How to move, rename, duplicate, export, print and delete notes and folders, and how to get them back.

## Move

Drag a row in the sidebar onto another row to put it inside; drop it near the top edge of a row to place it above instead. Or use **Move to…** (above an open item, or in a row's right-click menu) and pick where it goes. You can move into folders, projects and notes, but never into the item itself or something inside it.

Right-clicking a row offers the rest: add something inside it, **Rename** (also `F2`, or double-click the row), **Duplicate**, **Version history**, **Open on start** so it opens whenever Notible starts, and delete it. Exports and printing are under **Export and print**.

## Export

- **Download as Markdown** — the download button above an open item, or its right-click menu, saves it as a .md file.
- **Export JSON (with children)** — under **Export and print** in the right-click menu, saves an item with everything inside it as one file, to move it to another workspace or hand it to someone.
- **Print / Save as PDF** — under **Export and print** in the right-click menu.

## Import

- **Import Markdown** at the bottom of the sidebar turns a .md file into a note.
- **Import here…** in a container's right-click menu brings an exported JSON file back, inside that container.

## Delete and restore

The trash button above an item — or **Delete** in its right-click menu — moves it to the trash. Deleting a container moves everything inside it too.

Nothing is gone yet. Right after deleting, press **Undo** in the message at the top right. Later, open **Settings → Recovery → Trash**: **Restore** brings an item back where it was, **Delete forever** removes it for good, and **Empty trash** removes everything in the trash.

## Backups

Notible backs up your workspace when it starts, and keeps a set number of backups. Make one by hand, restore one, or change how many are kept in **Settings → Recovery**.
