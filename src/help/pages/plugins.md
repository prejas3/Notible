Much of Notible comes from plugins: tasks and projects, boards, planning, diagrams, writing tools. Notible itself is the notes, the tree, search and settings; plugins add the rest.

## Built-in plugins

These come with Notible and update with it. Some are on from the start; the others wait until you need them. Each has its own page in this chapter explaining what it adds.

## Turning plugins on and off

Open **Settings → Plugins**, pick a plugin to read what it does, and use its switch. Turning a plugin off hides what it adds — its tabs, blocks and menu entries — but your data stays, and comes back when you turn it on again.

Some plugins build on others; their page says which.

## More plugins

You can install more from the market. See [Market plugins](help:market-plugins).
