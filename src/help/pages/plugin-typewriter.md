A workspace for long-form writing — stories and books.

- **+ → Manuscript** creates a manuscript with four tabs: **Manuscript** (an outline of parts, chapters and scenes), **Codex** (characters, locations and plotlines, linked to the scenes they appear in), **Progress** (word targets, a deadline, a daily writing streak) and **Board** (the outline as cards).
- Scenes edit in a prose mode: a leading dash stays a dialogue dash instead of becoming a bullet, and the font can be set per manuscript or per scene. A **focus mode** button hides everything but the text.
- A finished draft compiles into a note, or exports as Word (.docx) or PDF.

**Off by default.**

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
