Adds planning tabs to projects:

- **Timeline** — a Gantt chart of dated tasks, milestones and cycles, with dependency lines;
- **Planning** — milestones, cycles and releases;
- **Calendar** — a month view of dated items;
- **Finance** — budget, actuals, forecast, time entries and weekly capacity. The costs, income and hours themselves come from the separate [Finance](help:plugin-finance) plugin; turn it on too.

Put a release date on the calendar, hang milestones off it, and see which slipping task moves the release.

**Off by default.** Needs [Tasks, Issues and Projects](help:plugin-work-models). Once it is on, choose which tabs a project shows under **Modules**.

[Open Settings → Plugins](settings:plugins)
