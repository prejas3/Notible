Folders, projects and workspaces are the three kinds of places made to hold other items, each built for something different. A note can hold items too, but it adds none of the organising described below.

![A folder, and a workspace holding projects, in the sidebar](help/containers-tree.webp)

## Folders

A folder keeps things together and imposes no structure: put notes, tasks, whiteboards or other folders in it, in any mix. Use a folder when you just want to keep things together.

## Projects

A project is built for managing work. It keeps reference notes separate from the work you track — tasks and issues — and shows that work in tabs such as a board, a table or a calendar.

A project shows its data in tabs. Choose which tabs it has under **Modules** at the right of the tab strip; turning a tab off hides it, and the data stays.

![A project's task board](help/project-board.webp)

## Workspaces

A workspace holds things and is also a dashboard. It lets you oversee several projects from one place: an overview of their progress, a table across all of them, and a shared whiteboard.

![A workspace overview](help/workspace-overview.webp)

## Which one should I use?

| | Use it for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Folder | Keeping related things together | A plain place, no rules |
| Project | Managing one piece of work | Notes plus tasks, issues and views in tabs |
| Workspace | Overseeing several projects | A dashboard across everything inside it |

You can nest them: a workspace can hold projects, and a project can hold folders.

## Moving and ordering

Drag a row in the sidebar onto another row to put it inside, or near a row's top edge to place it above. **Move to…** above an open item does the same from a list. With a container open, **+** creates new things inside it; the menu says where, and **Top level instead** puts them at the top.
