## Search

Click the magnifier next to **+**, or press `Ctrl+K` (or `Alt+Space`), and type. Every word you type must appear in what is found, in any order; part of a word is enough. Results come in groups:

- **Notes** (or **Results**, when tasks and other kinds are among them) — what matches in your workspace, with the folder or project it is in and the words around the match;
- **Settings** — the settings screen that matches, one click away;
- **Help** — the page and heading of this Help that matches.

## Filters

Click the **Filters** button at the end of the search box to narrow the results down by:

- **Kind** — only tasks, only notes, and so on;
- **Anywhere** — only inside one workspace, folder or project;
- **Tag** — only items with a given tag;
- **Dates** — only items changed between two dates.

**Clear filters** resets them.

## Backlinks

At the bottom of a note, **Backlinks** lists every note that links to it with `[[its title]]`, while **Backlinks and document stats** is on in Settings → Editor. See [Search and backlinks](help:plugin-search).
